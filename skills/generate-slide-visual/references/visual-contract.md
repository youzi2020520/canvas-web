# Visual Contract

Every request states: purpose, subject, environment, composition, camera or illustration treatment, lighting, palette, negative space, target aspect ratio, and consistency key. Reject generic decorative images that do not support the slide claim.

The slide image layer must use `objectFit: cover` or `contain`, an intentional `objectPosition`, and either a meaningful `clipPath` or at least 16px corner radius. Never stretch photography. Covers and case studies should normally reserve 35-55% of the canvas for the focal visual.
